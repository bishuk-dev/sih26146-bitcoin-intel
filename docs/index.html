DESIGN DECISION

Why hybrid, weighted toward unsupervised?

Isolation Forest answers “what is unusual?”. DBSCAN answers “which entities behave similarly?”. The graph answers “how are they connected?”. Labels generated by our synthetic engine are kept for evaluation only, not training.

Anomaly+Cluster+Graph+Network→Risk

JUDGE QUESTIONS

Know these answers cold

Why unsupervised?

Because reliable real-world labels are unavailable in the deployment setting; synthetic labels are kept for evaluation rather than training.

Does anomaly mean criminal?

No. It means the behavior deviates from the learned baseline and should be investigated.

Why a graph?

Because the core intelligence task is relational: how wallets, transactions and network observations connect.

Why not a huge neural model?

The system must run offline on CPU and explain its alerts. Simpler models make that constraint achievable and testable.
